Repair or replace?

The honest answer depends
on four things.

Factor Repair may make sense Replacement may make sense
Age

The roof is younger with useful life remaining

The system is at or near expected end of life

Damage

The issue is isolated to one repairable area

Wear or failure appears across multiple slopes

History

This is the first localized problem

Leaks or repairs have become recurring

Goal

Restore performance and extend the current roof

Reset long-term protection, appearance, and warranty

We consider all four together. Age alone does not condemn a roof, and one leak does not automatically justify replacement.
