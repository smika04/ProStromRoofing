Roof repair

Fix the cause. Keep good roofing in place.

Not every leak means a new roof. If the surrounding system is still sound, a focused repair can restore protection without replacing materials that still have useful life.

We commonly solve

  • Missing, lifted, cracked, or wind-damaged shingles
  • Leaks around chimneys, skylights, vents, and valleys
  • Failed flashing, exposed fasteners, and small punctures

What you receive

  • Leak tracing—not a guess based only on the ceiling stain
  • Before-and-after photo documentation
  • A defined repair plus notice of any larger risks we find

Inspection

Know what matters now. And what can wait.

An inspection replaces uncertainty with a practical priority list. It is useful before buying or selling, after severe weather, or when you simply do not know how much life the roof has left.

We examine

  • Surface wear, flashings, penetrations, valleys, and roof edges
  • Drainage, visible moisture paths, and ventilation warning signs
  • Previous repairs and areas likely to need future attention

You leave with

  • Photos and a clear explanation of current condition
  • Immediate, near-term, and monitor-only priorities
  • A maintenance, repair, or replacement recommendation if needed

Gutter cleaning

Keep water flowing. Protect your home.

Clogged gutters can cause water to overflow, damage exterior surfaces, and contribute to drainage problems around your foundation. Regular cleaning helps keep rainwater moving away from your home.

Good fit when

  • Leaves, moss, and debris are building up in the gutters
  • Water spills over the edges during rainfall
  • Downspouts appear blocked or drain slowly

Our scope includes

  • Removal of leaves and accumulated debris
  • Clearing accessible downspouts to support proper drainage
